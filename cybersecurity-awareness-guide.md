# Phishing awareness
- phishing is a cyberattack where a the attacker pretends to come from an organisation or a legitimate person and tricks one into giving out their personal information
## how to recognise a phishing attack
- attached links leading to unverified websites or pages 
- messages that puts u under pressure to update or verify one's account else it will be closed 
- request of personal information like passwords and usernames 
- unfamiliar/ unknown sources or sender like unfamiliar addresses of emails 
- poor spelling 
## What to do when receiving a suspicious email or message 
- confirm the source of the message 
- independently confirm the source to see it is a verified and secure source 
- don't click attached links or download attachments
- don't share any password or code until verified 
- report or delete suspicious messages 
# malware awareness 
a malware is a malicious software designed to disrupt the normal functioning of a computer and also damage and gain unauthorised access.
## damages caused by a malware 
- data loss
- causes the freezing of a device or cause it to be unresponsive
- malware like ransomware can encrypt data and the attacker would demand ransom in exchange of the decryption key
## ways to protect a device from malware 
- installation of an up-to-date anti-virus 
- regular updating of the compute's sftware 
- download files from verified and secure sources  
# password security 
- strong passwords are important as they will not be easy to be guessed by an attacker 
- a strong password is made up of uppercase, smallcase, numbers and special characters
-the strong password should be long and unique
- use different passwords for different accounts because if an attacker hacks one account they will try to used the same password they used for other accounts which means they will have access to all of your account if you used a the same passwords across all of your accounts 
# multi-factor authentication 
- this is an extra layer of security which is used when logging into your account instead of depending on a password only another form of verification will e needed which is where multi-factor authentication enters 
- multi-factor authentication is important because even when an attacker gets hold of your password they will need another verification 
- multi-factor authentication includes a one-time code sent to one's phone or an approval notification one the phone and we have facial and fingerprint recognition 
# safe internet browsing 
- one should stay protected when visiting websites when visiting public Wi-Fi and downloading information 
- clicking suspicious links should be avoided to prevent being directed  to being directed to fake or unverified websites designed to steal personal information or that spread malware 
- one should be careful when using public Wi-Fi because they may not be properly secured and other people who are accessing the public Wi-Fi can intercept information you are sending and also try to gain access to your device 
- one can protect themselves by avoiding sensitive transactions, using trusted and password-protected networks and lastly turning off file sharing when connected to these public networks
- one can check if a website is secure by; checking the website address it should start with HTTPS:// and not HTTP://, there should be a padlock sign next to the website's address, avoid suspicious pop ups   
# protecting personal information 
- personal information include full names, phone number, home address, password and bank details and if these are leaked they can expose one to identity theft, scams and cyberattacks.
- avoid sharing of personal information online to reduce risks of identity theft, scams and account hijacking 
- use of strong passwords and multi-factor authentication can be used to protect accounts 
-avoid sharing sensitive detail, becareful of the websites and apps u give your personal information to and set social media accounts to private and limit your audience
