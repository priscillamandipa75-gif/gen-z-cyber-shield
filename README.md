# gen-z-cyber-shield
A cybersecurity awareness guide designed to educate students about common online threats and safe digital practices and how to protect oneself when online.
## project goals 
- raise awareness of phishing and malware attacks
- encourage creation and use of strong passwords
- encourage and explain the importance of enabling of a multi-factor authentication
- use of safe, secure and trusted internet connections
# target audience 
main targets for this guide are the student and young adults 
## cybersecurity awareness topics covered 
1. online encountered threats/attacks
2. online protection
3. how to stay safe when online
4. importance of the cybersecurity awareness guide
5. how to handle challenges and attacks encountered when online
 ## importance of cybersecurity awareness guide 
 - protects personal information to prevent identity theft
 - creates good security habits which creates a safe and secure environment when online 
 - protects one from malware attacks which may lead to loss of important information 
 - encourages one to not let their guard down and prevent them from sharing their personal information which may later be used against them
